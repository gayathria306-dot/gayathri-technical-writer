About Me

5+ Domains
20+ Projects
2 Yrs Exp.

I'm a Technical Writer and Content Strategist working at the intersection of complex systems and human understanding. My focus is on industries where precision and clarity aren't nice-to-haves — they're critical: FinTech, Cybersecurity, AI/ML, API-first products, and developer-facing SaaS.

"Documentation isn't an afterthought — it's the product experience for every developer, analyst, or user who can't ask a question in real time."

I approach documentation as a systems problem: Who needs this? What do they need to accomplish? Where will they get stuck? My process combines structured information architecture with editorial sharpness — every document I produce is built to reduce friction and build trust at scale.

Whether it's an API reference guide for a developer portal, an incident response runbook for a SOC team, or a content strategy framework for a scaling product — I bring the same rigor: clarity of structure, precision of language, and an obsessive focus on the reader's experience.
